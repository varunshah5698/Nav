    Before you make an offer

    A quick gut check

    Budget past the mortgage

    Taxes, insurance, maintenance, and HOA dues can add 25–35% to the monthly number.

    Stress-test the rate

    Run the payment one or two points higher. If it still works, the budget is genuinely safe.

    Check the days on market

    Slower markets give you room to negotiate. Fast ones reward a clean, decisive offer.
